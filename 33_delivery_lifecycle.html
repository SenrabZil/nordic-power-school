01

The arc of one hour

Pick a single delivery hour — say 18:00 tomorrow. It is not decided at 18:00. It is shaped in layers, long before and long after: hedged months out, nominated in the day-ahead auction, repaired in intraday, balanced in real time, then settled and invoiced. Here is the whole arc on one line.

One delivery hour, decided in layers. The highlighted point is the hour itself; everything else happens before or after it.

Predict: at which stage is the price you actually receive for a last-minute deviation set?

Planning markets (forwards, day-ahead, intraday) lock what you arrange in advance. The deviation that remains is priced in real time — that is what Lessons 05, 19 and 29 are about.
02

What each layer does

Five layers, five jobs. None replaces another; together they decide the hour.

months – days out

Forwards & hedging

Lock a price for volume far ahead to manage risk, not to schedule a specific hour.

D−1, ~12:00 CET

Day-ahead auction

One clearing price per zone per hour. The backbone nomination for the delivery day.

D−1 → delivery

Intraday

Continuous trading and auctions to repair the plan as forecasts move, until gate closure.

real time

Balancing

The TSO activates reserves up or down to hold 50 Hz; the system’s direction sets the imbalance price.

after the fact

Settlement

eSett nets each BRP’s deviation at the imbalance price; invoicing and collateral follow.

03

Your exposure is not over at gate closure

A common trap: thinking the job ends when the intraday gate closes. It doesn’t.

Predict: intraday has closed for your hour. Are you done?

Gate closure ends your ability to trade the hour — not your exposure. Whatever your portfolio does physically after that is settled against you or for you in balancing.

05

The rule, in one breath

Forecast early, nominate day-ahead, repair intraday, balance in real time, settle after.

A delivery hour is decided in layers, not at the hour itself. Every lesson in this school lives at one of those layers — place it, and it makes sense.
