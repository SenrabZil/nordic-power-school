    Live is this, minus the hindsight

    Everything you just did, you did on a day that had already happened — so the market could confirm each call. That’s the replay engine: a recorded day, masked, revealed step by step.

    Phase 2 points the same engine at fresh publications instead of a recording. The reads are identical; the only difference is that the reveal hasn’t been printed yet. If you can read the replay, you can read today.

    This is reading practice on recorded data — not a signal, a recommendation, or a forecast you should act on. The school builds judgement; it does not place bids, trades or dispatch.

    ■

    The rule, in one breath

    Read the cues into the shape; the price is the consequence, not the start.

    Demand and wind give residual load; residual load gives the merit-order clear; the clear gives the spread; the spread, netted honestly, gives the trade; and finite power, stacked across the day, gives the plan. Read in that order and the day explains itself.

    That’s the reading method, proven on one day. The rest of the course adds the complications — geography, real time, assets — then one final hour, end to end.
