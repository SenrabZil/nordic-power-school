The arc

Offline first. Then live.

The curriculum is built and rehearsed on recorded history, where you can predict without consequence. Once the offline lessons are complete, the same engine points at live publications.

Phase 1 · now

Offline lessons

Recorded data, replayed. Predict-then-reveal, full provenance, zero risk. The place to build instinct.

Phase 2 · next

Live data

The same readers, pointed at fresh publications — re-fetching when the market prints, never streaming for its own sake.

Why it’s built this way

Four principles

The school has a spine. Every lesson honours it.

Predict, then reveal

You commit to a read before the answer uncovers. Guessing then checking is how instinct forms.

Offline & private

Lessons run on your machine. Nothing leaves it, and there’s nothing to log in to.

Provenance always

Every number carries where it came from — feed, fetch time, code version. You can always trace it.

No trade advice

This teaches you to read the market. It never tells you what to bid, trade, or dispatch.
