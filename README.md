# Nordic Power School

Offline, education-only lessons on **how Nordic & Baltic electricity prices form** — 32 interactive pages you open in a browser. Predict, then reveal. No install, no network, no trading.

Each lesson asks you to commit to a prediction before it shows you the answer, so you build the reasoning rather than just reading it. Everything runs locally from a single folder.

## Open it

Download or clone this repository, then open **`index.html`** in any modern browser. That's it — it works entirely offline, with no install, build step, or internet connection.

From there, start at Lesson 02 and follow the path in order, or jump to any lesson.

## View online

Because the course is plain static HTML, you can host it for free with **GitHub Pages**:

1. In this repository: **Settings → Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment → Source**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Set the branch to **`main`** and the folder to **`/ (root)`**, then **Save**.
4. Wait a minute, then open the published URL:
   `https://<your-username>.github.io/nordic-power-school/`

The published link opens the curriculum (`index.html`) directly.

## The curriculum

32 lessons, arranged as one dependency-ordered path. The curriculum page tiers them so not everything feels equally mandatory:

- **Core path** — the spine; follow in order.
- **Labs** — optional, hands-on interactives.
- **Professional extensions** — deeper topics you can take any time.

The reading order runs:

1. **Foundations: the market map** — time & zones, who does what, the Nordic/Baltic map, the delivery-hour lifecycle.
2. **The price engine** — price formation, hydro & water value, negative prices.
3. **Reading the market** — reading the replay, reading curves together, spreads & duration, a market-reading checkpoint.
4. **Grid, zones & borders** — zonal pricing, cross-zonal spreads, interconnectors & coupling, the Baltic dimension.
5. **From schedule to real time** — intraday market (+ lab), balancing, ancillary services, imbalance settlement (+ lab).
6. **Trading & assets** — forward curve, arbitrage, BESS value stacking, virtual power plants.
7. **Final capstone** — one delivery hour, end to end.
8. **Professional extensions** — ancillary markets, edge cases, algorithmic trading, carbon/EU ETS, capture price, storage/sun/wind.

The interconnector diagram on the landing page reflects the real Nordic & Baltic bidding zones (NO1–5, SE1–4, FI, DK1–2, EE, LV, LT) and their actual transmission links, including the cables to GB, DE and PL.

## Scope

This is a teaching resource about market *mechanics*. It is **education only**: it contains no bids, no trades, no dispatch instructions, and no buy/sell advice. It is not financial advice and not a trading tool.
