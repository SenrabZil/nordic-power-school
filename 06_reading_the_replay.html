Read the day

01

What replay is

The engine takes a real delivery day, hides the price, and walks it forward one print at a time. At each step you get only the knowable context — the forecasts that published before the gate — and you predict the print before lifting the mask.

Why this and not a live ticker? Because day-ahead doesn’t tick. It clears once, then prints. Replay on a recorded day and the live feed are the same loop — predict, reveal, learn — only the date differs. Master replay and you’ve already learned live.

02

Walk a day

Hour 00 is given. For each next hour you get the wind and demand cues; set your guess with the amber slider, then reveal. Your average error builds as you go — aim to read the shape, not nail every euro.

04

Replay is live, minus the date

The loop you just ran is the entire engine. Point it at a recorded day and it’s a lesson. Point it at the latest publication and it’s the live phase — the same predict-then-reveal, except the mask lifts when the market actually prints.

“Live” here means re-fetching when a new value publishes, gated by its publication time — not a stream of ticks. The gate, the published_at guard, the provenance on every print: identical to what you’ve been doing.

05

The rule, in one breath

Commit to a read before you lift the mask.

Use only what was knowable at the time, predict the print, then reveal and learn the gap. Calibration comes from doing this many times, on many days. The replay engine and the live feed are one loop — so when you’re ready for live, you already know the motion.
